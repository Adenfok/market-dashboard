How the score is computed (v2 — backtest-tuned)

Each indicator becomes a 0–100 favor score (higher = more fear / oversold / defensive → historically a better time to buy), then they're combined in a weighted average. Weights and mappings were tuned on 15 years (2011–26) and validated out-of-sample — see backtest.py / walkforward.py.
